Sweeper Shark is a private, local-first email threat scanner from Daemon OS.

Open an email message file and analyze it on-device for common phishing and spam signals, including urgent language, sensitive financial requests, suspicious links, and other recognizable patterns.

Sweeper Shark does not require an account, cloud upload, analytics SDK, ads, Google Play Services, or a mandatory network connection. The first release is intentionally small and transparent so the scanner can remain useful without sending messages anywhere.

Threat scores are explainable signals, not guarantees. Always verify unexpected requests through a trusted channel.
